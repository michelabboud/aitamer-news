# Brief (identical for every writer)

You are writing ONE post for aitamer.news, a news and explainer site about AI for working developers. Nobody will tell you who else is writing; write the best post you can.

## Rules

- Write ONE Markdown file: frontmatter in the site's format, then the body. Follow `POST.md` (the frontmatter contract) and `docs/posting-standards.md` (attribution, the wildness rating) in the working directory. Do not write `pubDate`, `specimen`, `heroImage` or `heroAlt`; no image is needed.
- `author: ari` is a placeholder for the test; keep it. `draft: true`.
- Every factual claim comes from a source you actually opened. Prefer primary sources (official docs, repositories, papers, release notes). Deep-link them in the body and list them all in `sources`. Never invent a number, a version, a price, a quote, a feature or a URL. If you cannot verify something, leave it out or say plainly that you could not verify it.
- Vendor claims are attributed ("X says ..."), not asserted.
- Plain, direct sentences, short paragraphs, headings that say what the section contains. Explain every acronym the first time. No hype words, no em-dash asides, no "not X but Y" framing, no rhetorical-question headings.
- Do not mention how you were asked to write the post, the test, other writers, or any tool you used.
- Your final reply is the complete file content only, nothing before or after it.
