# Scoring sheet

Score each post out of 100 without knowing which model wrote it. The key (post ID to model) is kept apart and opened only after every post is scored.

| Criterion | Points | What a full score looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | 30 | Every checkable claim is true and matches its source. Open the sources; the report lists each link's status. |
| Usefulness and depth | 20 | A reader could act on it; it covers what matters for the topic and length. |
| Honesty about uncertainty | 15 | Says what was not verified; vendor claims attributed; no invented specifics. |
| Structure and clarity | 15 | Plain sentences, headings that say what follows, acronyms explained, right length. |
| House voice and rules | 10 | Follows the brief's style rules; the report lists any broken rule. |
| Insight | 10 | Something a careful reader would not get from the vendor's own page. |

**Hard penalty:** an invented source, URL, number, quote or feature caps the post at 50, however good the rest is.

The report for each post holds only objective checks (word count, links resolving, format limits, style-rule hits). Everything else is your judgment.

Contestants: six models now; Foxy and Mai (both with memory) later, given the same three briefs, each of whom must agree first. Their posts go through the same anonymising and the same sheet, and the scores are read knowing that those two write from memory.
