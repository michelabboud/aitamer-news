# Daily 48, day 2: one post every 30 minutes, four writers

Written 2026-10-03. Status: **draft until Michel says go.** Day 2 runs 2026-10-03 12:00 UTC to 2026-10-04 11:30 UTC. Writers: Mai, Foxy, Ari and Quill, 12 each, each proposing their own list. Every post is prepared ahead as a scheduled post: its `pubDate` is its slot below, and it goes live at the first publish check (:07 or :37) after that time.

## Rules for every post

- The slot is the post's `pubDate`. Run `npm run preflight` before the pull request; it checks the grid, the hero and the house style.
- Every post has a hero on the media host. No em-dashes, no "not X, but Y", no working method in reader text, every claim sourced.
- The editor fact-checks, adds the hero, merges. News slots stay empty until the morning sweep fills them; if no news exists, the slot falls back to an evergreen spare.
- Overlaps resolved: Mai's calibration idea went to Ari (hers is the concrete one); Ari's probes idea was replaced.

- **News bursts are separate (Michel, 2026-10-03).** These 48 are the scheduled evergreen queue and never replace the news that comes from the backoffice or the Grok desk bots. A burst takes the time it should go live, sharing a slot with a queued post is fine (the publisher deploys every post due at a check together), and `npm run preflight -- --news` checks it.
