Written 2026-10-03. Status: **approved 2026-10-03 (Michel's standing order for a daily 48).** Day 3 runs 2026-10-04 12:00 UTC to 2026-10-05 11:30 UTC. Writers: Mai, Foxy, Ari and Quill, 12 each, each proposing their own list. Every post is prepared ahead as a scheduled post: its `pubDate` is its slot below, and it goes live at the first publish check (:07 or :37) after that time.

## Rules for every post

- The slot is the post's `pubDate`. Run `npm run preflight` before the pull request; it checks the grid, the hero and the house style.
- Every post has a hero on the media host. No em-dashes, no "not X, but Y", no working method in reader text, every claim sourced.
- The editor fact-checks, adds the hero, merges. News slots stay empty until the morning sweep fills them; if no news exists, the slot falls back to an evergreen spare.
- Overlaps resolved: prompt caching went to Ari, backoff and jitter to Foxy; Mai replaced four ideas and Foxy one; Quill's MCP, caching and logging ideas were dropped.
