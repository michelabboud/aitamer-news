Anthropic's [Claude for Startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups) page says approved founders get $1,000 in Claude API credits, a free year of Claude Team, and up to $45,000 in offer value from participating partners. The page says those benefits are subject to the [Startup Program Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms).

The FAQ is more specific. Claude Team is free for one year, for up to five Premium seats, and only if the organization is new to Team. A company that already has a Teams plan does not qualify. Every approved company gets $1,000 in API credits, which expire six months after they are granted, on the first-party Claude API through Claude Console. The FAQ says they cannot be used on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, or other third-party platforms.

The Claude Startup Stack is discounts and credits the page values at up to $45,000, from independent third parties, redeemed in the Console. Venture funding is not required. A company backed by a VC in Anthropic's partner network can also get up to $100,000 in additional API credits, claimed through that VC. Members who receive program credits also get higher API rate limits, the FAQ says.

## Who can apply, on the page and in the terms

The FAQ says startups founded in the last five years, or funded in the last two, can apply. The applicant needs a Claude Console account, a company email that matches the website's domain, and a short description of what the company is building. Anthropic says it reviews every application, and that applicants must comply with its supportability policies.

Applied AI office hours are 45-minute live sessions every other week, with a registration link by email and in the portal.

The terms page says "Last updated May 22, 2025." Eligibility is Anthropic's sole discretion. A company is not eligible if it is established or operating in, or plans to serve people in, Belarus, China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Sudan, Syria, Crimea, or the so-called Donetsk or Luhansk People's Republics, or if export controls bar it. Anthropic employees and their immediate families and household members are not eligible. The "$1,000" in those terms is a liability cap (program benefits awarded, or $1,000, whichever is less). It is not the credit amount. The $1,000 credit is on the program page. Existing members do not reapply. The FAQ says they can redeem the console offers.

## What the coverage adds

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-gives-startups-a-free-year-of-enterprise-service-and-1000-in-token-credits/), in a 6 October story by Russell Brandom at 9:00 a.m. PDT, reports the same credits, seats, and office hours. It quotes an Anthropic announcement: "We created this program because we believe the benefits of AI will reach most people through the companies that build on top of models, rather than through the models alone."

[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-claude-startups-program.html), by Ashley Capoot at 12:00 p.m. EDT on Tuesday, says the program launched in May and that thousands of companies are already participating, according to Anthropic. Beth Robertson, head of startups, told CNBC: "Startups are often the first to push Claude to its limits." CNBC calls the stack, in Anthropic's words, a "set of benefits for the tools a startup runs on." CNBC's own framing is that Anthropic has spent much of the last year trying to fend off rivals such as OpenAI and Google. That rivalry line is CNBC's.
