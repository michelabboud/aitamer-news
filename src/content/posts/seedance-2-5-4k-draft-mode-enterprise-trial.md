ByteDance has released a 4K version of its Seedance 2.5 video model. BytePlus, ByteDance's international cloud, [announced it on X](https://x.com/BytePlusGlobal/status/2113654442885292359) on 23 October, and Volcano Engine, its cloud in China, announced it the same day, as [IT Home](https://www.ithome.com/1/017/078.htm) and [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103406482.html) reported. BytePlus calls it "the most capable model in the Seedance 2.5 generation."

The catch comes in the same post: it is "currently available to selected enterprise customers," with a promise to "stay tuned for updates on API availability." There is no price and no date.

## What is new

- **4K output with 10-bit colour.** A [second BytePlus post](https://x.com/BytePlusGlobal/status/2113654445729034614) says it renders textures, contours and fine detail more fully, and credits 10-bit colour depth with richer gradations and smoother transitions "better suited to the big screen."
- **Draft mode.** You test storyboards and refine shots with lower-cost 480p previews, then generate the final version in 4K once a shot works. This is the most practical part of the launch: in video generation most takes get thrown away, so drafting them small is where the savings are.
- **Faces and small movements.** ByteDance says dedicated training improved facial expressions, subtle movements and visual detail. According to IT Home's report of the Volcano Engine launch, reference generation and video editing also keep the uploaded material's sharpness and colour better, including small text on posters and packaging.

BytePlus says the model's "screen debut" is a short film, *Otter is Nobody 2*, shown in its post.

## Who can use it

Per IT Home, Volcano Engine says some film and post-production companies in China and abroad have already tried it, and access "may" widen later. [Beijing News](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1792642976129728.html) reported on 22 October that the first trial batch is tied to specific productions and goes to 20 studios and directors worldwide. It quoted one studio as saying the pricing was not as high as it expected, because Draft mode lets you pay for 4K only on the keepers. That studio is not named, and the price itself is not public.

For comparison, the public Seedance 2.5 plans on the [BytePlus product page](https://www.byteplus.com/en/product/Seedance) list 480p and 720p resolution options and clips of 4 to 30 seconds. So 4K is not something ordinary BytePlus customers can buy today.

## What is missing

No sample clips beyond BytePlus's own film, no benchmark or arena score, no price per second, no maximum clip length at 4K and no API date. Everything about quality so far is ByteDance's own description.

## Practical takeaway

If you make video on Seedance 2.5 today, nothing changes yet. Borrow the idea anyway: draft takes at the lowest resolution your tool offers and spend on the high-resolution render only once a shot is locked. When BytePlus publishes 4K pricing, compare the cost of a 4K render with an upscaled 720p render of the same take before you switch.
