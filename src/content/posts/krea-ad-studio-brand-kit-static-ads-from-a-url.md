Krea launched [Ad Studio](https://www.krea.ai/blog/introducing-ad-studio) on 29 October and calls it the first "Krea 3 Expert," a guided tool built on its Krea 3 image model for one job: static ads for social placements.

## How it works

1. **Paste a URL.** Ad Studio reads the site and builds a brand kit: logo, colors, fonts, tone of voice, audience and selling points.
2. **Pick products.** It finds products on the site, including from a Shopify catalog, and one kit holds up to 20.
3. **Generate.** Choose placements: Instagram feed at 4:5 or 1:1, Stories and Reels at 9:16, or 16:9 landscape. It makes up to 12 ads per batch, one product per ad, with a mockup preview of how each looks in place.
4. **Edit.** Changes go through Krea Agent, Krea's chat-style editor, so you can ask for a different headline or background without starting over.

Krea's own walkthrough times the brand kit at 49 seconds and six Story ads at 2 minutes 28 seconds, about five minutes from URL to first ads. Those are demo figures, not independent tests.

## Price and limits

Ad Studio is on every paid plan from Basic up. Building the brand kit and importing products are free; the ads themselves use compute units, Krea's credit system. The output is still images only, with no video ads.

## Why this matters

Making the same campaign in five aspect ratios is slow work for small shops, and this packages the brand research and resizing into one flow. It is also a sign that image-model companies are building finished task tools on top of their models, as Krea did after opening [Krea 3 editing on its API](/posts/krea-3-image-editing-beta-api/) and as [Canva and Figma are doing with their own editing models](/posts/canva-brush-figma-post-train-own-design-editing-models/). Before running these ads, check every price, claim and product detail, because the copy is generated.
