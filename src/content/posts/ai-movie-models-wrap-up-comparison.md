Artificial Analysis (AA) ranks below were read on 23 November 2026, from its text-to-video and image-to-video boards **with audio**. Prices for Grok Imagine, Runway, MiniMax H3 and Marey were rechecked on the vendor or host page the same day. A cell marked "ep." comes from that episode's sources and was not rechecked. Both boards move every week, and neighbours are often inside each other's error bars.

## What moved

**Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash climbed.** Google [made 1.2 Flash generally available on 6 November](/posts/google-gemini-omni-1-2-flash-ga/) at $0.07 a second at 720p. On 23 November it was sixth for text-to-video (Elo 1153) and 15th for image-to-video. The 1.1 model we covered in episode 2 sits 15th and 20th.

**The leaders held.** Wan 3.0 still tops text-to-video at 1174, with Utopai X second at 1170 and MiniMax H3 (768p) third at 1163. Seedance 2.5 still tops image-to-video at 1162. The top three text-to-video models overlap inside AA's ranges, so treat them as a tie.

**Newcomers without an episode.** Hyperflow H3 is fourth for text-to-video, with its API "coming soon" on AA. Boreal-H3 is third for image-to-video. FLUX 3 Max, from the image lab, is seventh for text-to-video. We have not reviewed any of them.

**Grok Imagine slipped on AA, prices unchanged.** SpaceXSI's price list still charges $0.08, $0.14 and $0.25 a second at 480p, 720p and 1080p for Video 1.5, and $0.02, $0.03 and $0.14 for 1.5 Lite, plus $0.01 per input image. Episode 3 had Video 1.5 at 12th. It is 17th for text-to-video (1049) and 19th for image-to-video now. The docs still say text-to-video on 1.5 runs native 1080p.

**Kling 4.0 is still not ranked.** AA lists only Kling 3.0, which is the board's fixed 1,000-point anchor. The 4.0 API price in our [Kling 4.0 news story](/posts/kling-4-0-general-availability/) was $0.157 a second at 720p; we could not reread Kling's price table on 23 November, so that cell is marked ep.

**Unchanged on recheck:** Runway still bills Gen-4.5 at 12 credits a second, at $0.01 a credit. MiniMax still lists H3 at $0.08 a second at 768p and $0.13 at 2K. fal.ai still lists Marey at $1.50 for 5 seconds and $3.00 for 10.

## Our verdict

This is opinion. For text-to-video with sound, start with Wan 3.0 or MiniMax H3: both are in AA's top three, and H3 is $0.40 per 5 seconds at 768p against Wan's $1.00 at 1080p. For image-to-video, Seedance 2.5 leads but is among the priciest at about $2.84 per 5 seconds; MiniMax H3 and HeyGen Video sit fifth and sixth for much less. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is the new mid-price all-rounder. Grok Imagine Video 1.5 gives native 1080p and nine aspect ratios at $0.08 to $0.25 a second but sits 17th on AA, so it is not a leaderboard pick; 1.5 Lite at $0.02 a second at 480p is the cheap drafting option. Pick Marey only when licensed training data is the requirement. For your own GPU, MiniMax H3 and LTX-2.5 are the strongest open weights here, but read their licences first.

## Why this matters

Video prices still span more than ten times between rows that sit close together on AA, so the board alone will not pick a model for a budget. HeyGen's promotional price ends on 30 November, per its episode, so budget at the December rate. Kling 4.0, Runway Gen-4.5, Luma Ray3.2 and Marey still have no AA rank, so test them on your own prompts before you compare them with ranked models. As with the [image wrap-up](/posts/ai-image-models-wrap-up-comparison/), write down the date of any price or rank you rely on.
