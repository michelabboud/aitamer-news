OpenAI says GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage was counted too cheaply for most users after launch, and that it has now fixed the mistake. In a [post on X](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2125784147889393806) on 26 November, Tibo, whose profile says he works on Codex and ChatGPT at OpenAI, said the same work will now use more of a plan's usage allowance.

## What changes

Compared with last week, OpenAI says you will reach your limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on GPT-6.2 Sol. It says both should still go further than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol did. Other models were not affected, and OpenAI says it will not charge for the difference on past usage.

As an apology, OpenAI says it will add one banked reset for everyone on the Go, Plus, Pro and Business plans. The first post did not say when it would appear; at 07:26 UTC on 26 November, Tibo [followed up](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2125853628250599730) that the "banked reset is online". Neither post says how long the reset lasts.

## Context

We covered [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](/posts/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) on 17 November, including lower Astra API prices. This change is about plan usage allowances; the post does not mention API pricing. It also lands shortly before ChatGPT and Codex move to [one shared, token-based allowance in December](/posts/chatgpt-codex-shared-usage-allowance-december/).

## Why this matters

If you planned this week's Codex work around how far GPT-6.2 went in its first week, those numbers no longer hold: by OpenAI's own figure, the same tasks on Astra now use your cap about 40% faster. Before a long agent run, check where your plan's usage meter stands, remember that Sol's increase is smaller at 25%, and save the banked reset for a heavy day once it appears.
