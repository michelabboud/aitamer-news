South Korea plans a 4.7 trillion won program to develop a frontier AI model from March 2027, the science ministry said, according to a Reuters story datelined Seoul on 6 October. Reuters.com did not return the story. The text below is the Reuters copy on [StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/South+Korea+plans+to+develop+%243.5+billion+frontier+AI+model+starting+next+year/27150630.html), which prints the "SEOUL, Oct 6 (Reuters)" dateline, the byline Joyce Lee, and the editor credit Ed Davies. StreetInsider's timestamp is 5 October 2026, 10:21 p.m. Eastern.

## What the Reuters copy says

The wire says the Ministry of Science and ICT plans a competitive tender after parliament approves the 2027 budget in December, with a winner potentially as early as February. The government plans to combine state equity with private funding and to concentrate chips, data, and talent. Participation is open to single companies, consortiums, or special-purpose vehicles, and the ministry said it aimed to let startups, firms, universities, and researchers play leading roles.

The same copy says the initiative would be separate from the home-grown foundation model program, which will continue and select two final teams in a third-stage review in February 2027.

Reuters prints the budget as "4.7 trillion won ($3.5 billion)" and, at the bottom, "($1 = 1,344.5000 won)." That dollar figure and that rate are Reuters'. This article does not convert won on its own. Reuters frames the plan as Seoul seeking to compete in the race to build the most advanced artificial intelligence. That compete-in-the-race line is Reuters' framing of the ministry's announcement.

The Reuters copy does not mention a GPU count, a Vera Rubin purchase, or an 800 billion won data line.

## The GPU line is in other outlets

[The Asia Business Daily](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026100512521272259), in an English article by Seo Sojeong dated 5 October and updated 6 October, says that according to the ministry's budget proposal for next year, released on 1 September, 4.7 trillion won is allocated to frontier AI model development. It then says 3.9 trillion won is set aside to purchase 10,000 "Vera Rubin" GPUs, 800 billion won for AI training data, and 25 billion won to attract 20 researchers from abroad. Those three lines are Asia Business Daily's account of the September proposal. They are planned budget items. The article does not say the GPUs have been delivered.

[Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/05/korea-to-keep-sovereign-ai-model-project-adds-frontier-ai), an AI translation of reporting by Cho Yoon-jin, prints 4.7 trillion won ($3.3 billion) and 10,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin GPUs along with data. The $3.3 billion is that outlet's dollar figure. It says the call is planned around the end of the year, when the budget bill clears the National Assembly, with development as early as March.

[The Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10893306) prints 4.7 trillion won ($3.49 billion) as proposed equity investment, with private capital expected on top, still subject to National Assembly approval. The Herald's $3.49 billion, Seoul Economic Daily's $3.3 billion, and Reuters' $3.5 billion are three labels.

## What happens to the existing program

Reuters says the home-grown program continues, selects two final teams in a third-stage review in February 2027, and stays separate from the frontier project.

The Herald says teams led by LG AI Research, SK Telecom, and Upstage continue, with two finalists. It quotes Ryu Je-myung: "We will select two. That is certain." It also says continued computing support through 2027 will not be automatic: a new frontier competition replaces that extension. The Herald, not the Reuters copy, reports his view of rivals. He called head-on competition with the largest U.S. AI companies unrealistic, and said Korea could aim at leading Chinese open models if the planned investment materializes.

[Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/110729/korea-to-keep-support-for-two-domestic-ai-foundation-model-teams-to-2027-frontier-ai-tender-possible-in-december), published 5 October, says the ministry will select two teams in February and keep the planned fourth round and GPU support unchanged through 2027. That line does not match the Herald.

The budget remains a proposal until parliament acts. The 10,000 GPUs are a planned purchase in the outlets that mention them.
