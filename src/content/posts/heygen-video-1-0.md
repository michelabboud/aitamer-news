HeyGen Help (**Last Updated September 30, 2026**) introduces **HeyGen Video**—the company’s **first general-purpose** video model for cinematic clips from a prompt, **without requiring an avatar** ([Help](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/17272995-introducing-heygen-video-generate-cinematic-clips-from-a-prompt)).

This is a gen-video product story (text / image / reference → clip), not an avatar-conversation or deepfake-research lane.

## Modes: T2V, I2V, Ref2V

Per Help:

- **Text to Video (T2V):** describe a scene; get a clip with **synchronized dialogue, ambience, and sound effects** generated in the **same call**.
- **Image to Video (I2V):** drop in a reference image; the model animates it into a clip.
- **Reference to Video (Ref2V):** provide a reference video to guide motion, composition, or style. Billing covers **input video seconds + output video seconds**; **image and audio references are free**.

## Availability and channels

HeyGen says the model is **available via API right now**; **web platform access is coming soon**—do not call web GA ([Help](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/17272995-introducing-heygen-video-generate-cinematic-clips-from-a-prompt)).

Named access paths: **HeyGen API**, **OpenRouter** (primary launch partner), **Runware**, and **ComfyUI**.

Runware’s companion card lists model ID **`heygen:video@1.0`**, status **live**, release **September 30, 2026**, and outputs of **5 to 15 second** clips at **480p or 768p** ([Runware](https://runware.ai/docs/models/heygen-video-1-0.md)).
