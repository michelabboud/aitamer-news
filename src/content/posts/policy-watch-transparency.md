Transparency requirements are no longer a vague “nice to have.” Product teams shipping assistants, agents, or generative features should assume users (and auditors) will ask:

- Was this content machine-generated or human-edited?
- What model or system produced it?
- Where can someone contest an automated decision?

At AI Tamer we treat the **AI / Human** badge as editorial hygiene, not decoration. The same habit helps product UX: label machine output early, keep an audit trail, and do not bury disclosures in footers.
