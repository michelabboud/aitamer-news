Supabase’s blog on **October 2, 2026** announces that it is **acquiring Turso**—Turso is joining Supabase to build database infrastructure for **agent-scale, on-demand databases** ([blog](https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-is-acquiring-turso)). The post does **not** state a deal price, equity terms, or close date.

## What stays the same (engines)

Supabase’s continuity line is explicit: **Supabase will continue building around Postgres, while Turso will continue its work on SQLite.** This is not framed as a forced single-engine migration or an end to either product. Supabase also says that **for existing users, nothing changes**—a **vendor assurance**, not a newsroom SLA guarantee.

## Why Turso (architecture)

Supabase describes Turso’s path as a **Rust-rebuilt SQLite** and a cloud design where a single server can manage **millions of databases**, loading when needed and suspending when not—suited to provisioning per-agent databases on Turso Cloud or in customer clouds. Named examples in the post include **Superhuman, Sauna.ai, CTO.new, and Mastra** running that pattern—**nominative, Supabase-attributed**, not partnership exclusivity claims.

Separately, Supabase says it is already **launching over one million databases per week**—a **Supabase-attributed** scale figure, not independently verified here.

## Who joins and who leads

Supabase welcomes **Glauber Costa** and **Pekka Enberg** with the rest of the Turso team. **Glauber will lead the agentic infrastructure effort** at Supabase. Roadmap language about a path into the broader Supabase ecosystem as workloads grow stays vision-level; the post does not ship a merge timeline or product rename GA.

## Who should care

Teams on Supabase Postgres, Turso SQLite, or agent stacks that spin up many short-lived databases should read the [announcement](https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-is-acquiring-turso) for the continuity and leadership framing—and wait for contractual/product detail beyond “nothing changes” before treating plan terms as locked.
