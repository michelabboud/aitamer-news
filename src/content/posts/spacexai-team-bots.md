SpaceXAI launched **Team Bots**—shared **Grok Bots** built around a role or workflow, then shared so the whole team works from the same expertise ([SpaceXAI](https://x.ai/news/team-bots)). The company says they are **available today in public beta on Teams and Enterprise plans**.

This is a **shared workplace teammate** surface: one Bot with team-wide skills, private one-to-one chats, and a Slack handle—not a consumer personal agent, not a phone receptionist, and not a BI-agent console.

## Context, plugins, credentials, memories

Every Team Bot, as SpaceXAI frames it, brings four surfaces together:

- **Context** — files, instructions, and skills (brand guides through internal docs)
- **Plugins** — apps such as Salesforce, Notion, and GitHub (per person or team-configured)
- **Credentials** — secure access to third-party APIs that lack a plugin
- **Memories** — retain what the Bot learns and improve at the role over time

The Bot is shared; **each person’s conversations remain private**. SpaceXAI says the Bot keeps separate context and memories per user while drawing on skills shared across the team.

In Slack, each Team Bot has **its own handle**—invite it to a channel so everyone can ask questions, contribute context, and see responses ([SpaceXAI](https://x.ai/news/team-bots)).

## Four role examples (vendor narrative)

SpaceXAI walks through how it uses Team Bots internally:

1. **Sales / Customer Success** — account bots shared by AE, CSM, SA, and sales lead; overnight review of news, Gong, Notion, and Slack; morning channel briefings with role-tailored drafts
2. **Product and Engineering** — Notion, Linear, Hex, Datadog, and Cursor; launches **Cloud Agents**; PR-review skills; Slack progress reports
3. **Marketing** — brand guidelines and copy; Slack draft review; website and SEO preview-to-sign-off flow
4. **Data Analytics** — shared **read-only credentials** to approved **Databricks** tables, plus Datadog / Hex / Statsig for investigation

Treat these as **vendor worked examples**, not independent newsroom case studies.

## Customer and internal color (attributed)

Harper CEO **Dakotah Rice** is quoted saying the company built a Team Bot in 24 hours for lapsed-policy recovery, “saving our customers over $120,000 from hundreds of policies.” Amplitude Head of Marketing **Angela Ferrante** says the company is “building toward Team Bots for every marketing function.” SpaceXAI also describes an internal five-person engineering setup that “ship[ped] more than 100 PRs a day” while building Team Bots, and a data skills library covering “more than 45,000 tables” handed to Data Bot. All of that is **SpaceXAI-attributed vendor color**—not independently verified here ([SpaceXAI](https://x.ai/news/team-bots)).
