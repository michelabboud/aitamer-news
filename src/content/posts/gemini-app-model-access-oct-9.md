## Update, 10 October 2026: free accounts get Auto, and the Deep Think line is gone

Google has revised the [help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). When we opened it at 01:52 UTC on 10 October, the row for users without a plan no longer showed Flash-Lite alone. It reads "Auto" across all three model columns. "Auto will be the default setting for users without a Google AI plan," the page says. "Most prompts will go to Flash-Lite, our fastest model. Prompts that need deeper reasoning may go to Flash or Pro." Flash-Lite for everything now applies only with smart model selection off: "If smart model selection is turned off, chats for users without an AI plan will use Flash-Lite for all responses."

The other rows are unchanged. AI Plus has Flash-Lite and Flash with an X under Pro, and AI Pro and AI Ultra have all three. The 9 October start for accounts without a subscription, the email for AI Plus, and the low, medium and high effort tip are all still on the page.

The Deep Think sentence quoted below, "If you have an AI Ultra subscription you'll also have access to the Deep Think option for maximum parallel reasoning," was not on the page at that check. Deep Think now appears only in the May "Previous changes" list of premium features that use limits faster. The page no longer says which plan gets Deep Think.

[9to5Google reported](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/09/gemini-auto-free-users/) at 01:24 UTC on 10 October that the change is rolling out now. It says the model picker for free users shows only "Auto," and that the change reached most of its free test accounts on the evening of 9 October, Pacific time. It also reports new low, medium and high thinking levels that replace the previous "Extended" option, rolling out to subscribers. The help page calls these effort levels and does not mention "Extended" outside its May section.

The original report below describes the help page as it read on 6 October.

Google's Gemini Apps help page, [Changes to Gemini model access and limits](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136), says that starting in October 2026 model availability will change in Gemini Apps on a personal account. These changes "will start to take effect for users without an AI subscription on October 9th." For AI Plus, "you should receive an email that explains when these changes will take effect for you." 9 October 2026 is a Friday.

The page names Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro, and it does not print version numbers. [Abner Li's 3 October story at 9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/03/gemini-model-limits-oct-26/) adds version numbers. Its URL contains "oct-26". The body says October 9. This article uses the help page's names and that Friday date.

## What each plan keeps

On 6 October, the table was checkmark and X images:

| Google AI plan | Flash-Lite | Flash | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Without a plan | Check | X | X |
| AI Plus | Check | Check | X |
| AI Pro | Check | Check | Check |
| AI Ultra | Check | Check | Check |

In that version, without a plan, Flash-Lite was the only model once the change applied. The 10 October version replaces that row with Auto (see the update above). AI Plus keeps Flash-Lite and Flash, and the Pro cell is an X. AI Pro and AI Ultra keep all three. The page says you can subscribe, upgrade, change, or cancel at any time, and it says to update the Gemini mobile app.

The help page prints no prices. On the [Gemini subscriptions page](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/), opened the same morning with United States selected, AI Plus is $4.99 a month, AI Pro is $19.99 a month, and AI Ultra starts at $99.99 a month, with a second Ultra price of $199.99 a month.

## Effort, Deep Think, and older limits

For each available model, the help page says you can select effort of low, medium, or high. Higher effort, it says, does more thorough work and uses more of the limit. 9to5Google reads the levels as an October addition.

Deep Think is separate. On 6 October the help page said: "If you have an AI Ultra subscription you'll also have access to the Deep Think option for maximum parallel reasoning." 9to5Google's headline says AI Pro is adding Deep Think, and the story gives that option to the $19.99 plan, describing it as currently on the $99.99 and $199.99 plans. The help page opened on 6 October named Ultra only. The United States subscriptions page also puts "first access" to features "like Deep Think" on Ultra. As of 10 October the help page no longer carries the Ultra sentence and names no plan for Deep Think.

Under "Previous changes," the same page says a limits shift started on 17 May 2026 for users over 18 and on 24 July 2026 for users under 18. Gemini moved to compute-based limits that refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit. The calculation uses prompt complexity, features, and chat length. Paid users have higher limits. Premium use, including media generation, Deep Research, the Pro model, extended thinking, and Deep Think, spends the limit faster.

| Plan | Limit in that May section |
| --- | --- |
| Without a plan | Standard limits |
| AI Plus | 2x higher than standard limits |
| AI Pro | 4x higher than standard limits |
| AI Ultra | 5x or 20x higher than AI Pro, depending on the subscription |

The United States price page maps those Ultra multiples to $99.99 a month (5 times AI Pro) and $199.99 a month (20 times). That table is the May section of the help page.

## Before Friday

As of 6 October, the table removed Flash and Pro from personal accounts with no AI subscription from 9 October. The 10 October page instead routes those accounts through Auto, where harder prompts may still reach Flash or Pro but you cannot pick them. To choose a model yourself, pick a plan from the table.

AI Plus should wait for the email. After the change, that row is Flash-Lite and Flash, with an X under Pro. On the United States page, AI Pro, at $19.99 a month, is the listed plan that still includes Pro. AI Plus is $4.99.

Higher effort spends the limit faster. On 6 October the help page listed Deep Think for Ultra; on 10 October it no longer names a plan.

[Skills replace Gems](https://aitamer.news/posts/gemini-skills-replace-gems/) on another date. The Gemini app rollout cited there is 13 October, separate from this table.

## Practical takeaway

Match the help page to your plan. Since 9 October, accounts without an AI subscription get Auto, mostly Flash-Lite. AI Plus waits on an email. For Deep Think, the live page no longer names a plan.
