## Ask Studio (Studio AI)

Per the YouTube Blog, Ask Studio now covers more of the creation journey:

- **Ask Studio feedback** — personalized advice on pacing, structure, storytelling (and more) for drafts in YouTube Studio
- **Insights** and **Research** destinations — past performance plus outlier videos to spark next ideas
- **Channel-matched / generated thumbnails** — thumbnails matched to channel style
- **Dynamic thumbnails** — recommend the best of three options to different audience segments
- **Background Ask Studio on older videos** — suggest and test refreshed thumbnails for classics
- **Video A/B testing (coming soon)** — test up to three cuts/hooks; builds on title/thumbnail A/B testing (YouTube cites **40M+** title/thumbnail experiments since the official 2024 launch)

## Gemini conversational editing

YouTube says Gemini (including prior Gemini Omni momentum in Shorts/Create; hundreds of thousands of channels/day cited for Aug 2026) powers a **new conversational editing assistant** for **Shorts** and the **YouTube Create** app. Flow: suggested or custom prompts → first draft → chat to reorder frames, trim, sync music to beat, and more, bouncing between the AI partner and the manual timeline.

**Availability nuance:** the primary blog announces the integration. **TechCrunch** reports **early 2027** availability for Shorts + Create ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/youtubes-conversational-video-editing-tool-lets-creators-make-edits-in-natural-language/)) — that timing is **TechCrunch-attributed**, not YouTube Blog wording. Secondary also notes natural-language take selection, transitions, text overlays, and pause removal, with an on-stage demo by creator Happy Kelli.

YouTube’s GenAI trends report, cited in the blog: **72%** of U.S. video posters ages 14–44 used AI to create/edit in the past year.
