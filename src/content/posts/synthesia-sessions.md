Synthesia launched **Sessions** (post **2026-10-01**): a ready-made platform for two-way conversations with lifelike Interactive Avatars—practice, insight gathering, and (soon) expert guidance—alongside its existing enterprise video stack ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform), [Sessions](https://www.synthesia.io/sessions)).

## What’s live vs coming soon (HARD)

**Live today (Oct 1, 2026):** **Roleplay Sessions** and **Survey Sessions** ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform), [Sessions](https://www.synthesia.io/sessions)).

**Coming soon only — never GA in this copy:** **Expert Sessions** (Avatar that knows your product/processes guides step-by-step). Do not call Expert Sessions generally available ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform)).

**Soft lineage:** Roleplay existed earlier for customers (~July 2026 era)—do **not** invent the entire Sessions suite as brand-new on Oct 1; Survey is called out as a brand-new Session type launching today ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform)).

## Roleplay + Survey (as Synthesia describes)

**Roleplay:** practice sales discovery, manager feedback, support escalations, etc., with an Avatar that responds/pushes back; coaching and scoring; personalized scenarios and custom roleplays ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform)).

**Survey:** Avatar asks questions one-to-one, follows up, returns structured insight—depth of interview at form scale (onboarding check-ins, customer problems, lead qual) ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform)).

## Soft stats + free try

Synthesia-stated early results: **78%** of learners returned to attempt a roleplay more than once; of those, **83%** improved their score on a later attempt—**vendor-attributed only** ([blog](https://www.synthesia.io/post/sessions-interactive-avatar-platform)).

FAQ soft: you can try Roleplay or Survey **for free, no credit card required** ([Sessions](https://www.synthesia.io/sessions)).

## Who should care

L&D, sales enablement, and research teams evaluating Avatar-led practice/interviews should start at [synthesia.io/sessions](https://www.synthesia.io/sessions)—keep **Roleplay+Survey live / Expert coming-soon**, soft-attribute the 78%/83% figures, and don’t treat Expert as shipped.
