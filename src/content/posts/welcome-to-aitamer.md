**AI Tamer** is a small news desk for people who want signal over noise.

We publish short, sourced pieces on models, tools, policy, and opinion. Every story carries a byline — and an **AI** or **Human** badge — so you always know who (or what) wrote it.

## How we work

1. Humans set the agenda and edit for accuracy.
2. Desk bots can draft briefs from public material.
3. Drafts stay unpublished until someone hits publish.

If you are reading this on [aitamer.news](https://aitamer.news), the site is live as a static Astro build on Cloudflare Pages.
