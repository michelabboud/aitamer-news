On 13 November, Google [added Google Wallet](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/google-wallet-gemini-app/) to the apps Personal Intelligence can connect in the Gemini app. The post says this is rolling out to all Gemini app users in the United States, on the web and on Android and iOS. You tag @Google Wallet. Google's examples are a spending chart, a search for subscriptions that went up in price, and a holiday budget from last year's gifts and travel. Linking a bank or credit card to Wallet through Plaid is optional, and Google says that is needed for "deeper insights."

[Plaid's post](https://plaid.com/blog/google-wallet-gemini-plaid/) the same day adds loans and investment accounts to banks and credit cards, and says those connections can be managed in Plaid Portal. Google's post names credit cards and banks.

## What to check before you connect

The practical controls are in Google's help pages, including the Connected Apps page the blog links.

The blog says the connection is off until you opt in, under Connected Apps in Personal Intelligence settings, and that you can turn it off at any time. If you connect, "Gemini can only read information from your accounts; it can't move, manage, or invest your money." Plaid uses the same read-only line: Gemini cannot move, manage, or invest the money.

The [connect help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598406?hl=en) says Personal Intelligence requires you to be 18 or over, signed in with a personal Google Account, with Keep Activity on. It is not available on a work, school, or supervised account. For now it is also unavailable in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Nigeria, and it is unavailable in some Gemini features, including Gems. Wallet itself is listed there as a US-only connected app, covering payment methods, receipts, data from Google services saved in Wallet, and financial account info linked with services such as Plaid. The blog's "all Gemini app users in the U.S." sits next to those limits. Neither the 13 November post nor that help page names Google AI Pro or Ultra as a requirement for Wallet.

Disconnect Wallet under Personal Intelligence, then Connected Apps: off in the mobile app, or Disconnect on the web. That does not delete Gemini Apps activity, so delete those chats too if you want them gone. Deleting data in Wallet or a bank feed may take days to show up in Gemini. Drop the bank link in Plaid Portal.

On training, the [Connected Apps help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988?hl=en) has to be read as a pair. If Keep Activity is on, Gemini Apps activity, including data from connected apps, is used to improve Google services, including training generative AI models. The same page says Gemini does not train those models directly on Google Wallet and payments services. It does say that summaries, excerpts, and inferences from relevant transactions may be used to answer you, and that Google trains on those summaries and inferences. In some cases, it says, a summary of a short email or file is the email or file. Personal Intelligence requires Keep Activity, so that branch applies while Wallet is connected. Turn Keep Activity off to stop future chats being used to improve Google AI. A subset of connected-app data can be reviewed by people, including trained service providers. The page says not to connect apps that hold anything you would not want a reviewer to see, and not to rely on Gemini for financial advice.

Memory Summary is a separate editable list of saved preferences.

## Why this matters

Decide the privacy steps before you tag @Google Wallet: personal account, 18 or over, Keep Activity, and a plan for disconnecting both in Gemini and in Plaid Portal. [Claude Personal Finance](/posts/anthropic-claude-personal-finance-plaid-us-beta/) is the nearer product: a US beta on Pro, Max, and Ultra, read-only through Plaid, with Anthropic saying chats in that hub are not used to train its models. Google's Wallet answer is the split above, on Gemini app users in the US who meet the help-page limits, and the pages we read do not name a paid Gemini plan. [Search has a different Personal Intelligence connection](/posts/did-you-know-google-personal-intelligence-ads/), where Google has said inferences from Gmail and Photos can shape ads. Turning Wallet off in the Gemini app does not change that Search setting.
