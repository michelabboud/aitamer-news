IBM’s announcement **Published 30 September 2026** says **IBM Content Cortex Premium** is **now generally available**, bringing governed AI agents for **redaction**, **legal holds**, **classification**, and **search** into the enterprise content lifecycle ([IBM announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap)).

## Highlights

As IBM names them, Premium’s purpose-built agents cover:

- **Redaction**
- **Legal holds**
- **Classification**
- **Extraction of key data** / metadata
- **Semantic / smart search** (metadata + keyword + semantic; with Content Assistant, IBM describes a verified answer with cited source excerpts)

They run **directly within the content lifecycle**—in **folders**, **search results**, and **document viewers** ([IBM announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap)).

## Policies, human review, audit

IBM says the agents evaluate **explicit policies the organization configures**—not generic definitions of sensitive content—covering what to redact, permanent vs role-based redaction, when to lift a legal hold, and similar rules ([IBM announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap)).

For **high-impact** actions, IBM describes **human-in-the-loop** review: the agent recommends next steps (with a **confidence score**) and routes work to a human reviewer for **sign-off**. The **request**, **recommendation**, **approval**, and **resulting changes** land in an **immutable audit log**. Treat redaction, holds, retention, audit, and “defensible disposition” wording as **IBM product framing**—not a claim that any named statute or regulator is satisfied.

## Governance on AI representations

IBM says access permissions, retention rules, lineage tracking, and defensible disposition also apply to **AI representations**—**summaries**, **AI-generated metadata**, and **embeddings**—not only the original documents ([IBM announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap)).

## Edition, upgrade path, scale (IBM-attributed)

IBM frames **Essentials** around whether AI can safely analyze and act; **Premium** adds specialized agents alongside the **Navigator Core Agent**, sharing the same governance / policy / audit model as IBM describes. **FileNet Content Manager** investments carry forward by entitlement via **native compatibility APIs**; **CMEE** / **CMOD** get a supported, phased transition—IBM’s upgrade-path marketing, not an independent migration guarantee. Scale claims (“tens of billions of documents,” “many petabytes”) are **IBM-attributed only**. The primary publishes **no pricing or SKUs**; this brief invents none ([IBM announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap)).

## Not coding agents, not Kong Context

This is **governed document / content lifecycle** agents on Content Cortex—not IBM coding or watsonx developer-agent stories, and not **Kong Context Management**.

## Who should care

Enterprise content, records, and legal-ops teams evaluating governed agents inside folders, search, and viewers—under customer-configured policies with human sign-off and an audit trail as IBM describes—should start at the [IBM Content Cortex Premium announcement](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-content-cortex-closing-the-data-value-gap).
