Microsoft says **Teams Phone Agent** **begins rolling out to general availability** as of **2026-09-30** (JohnnyMcCormick on the Teams Blog; page updated **2026-10-01**): an **AI-powered calling receptionist** on customer-facing **Teams Phone** service lines, so callers can say what they need instead of working a phone menu ([blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftteamsblog/help-your-customers-get-answers-and-support-with-teams-phone-agent/4560840); [setup](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-setup-teams-phone-agent)).

This is Microsoft’s Teams Phone receptionist product—not third-party speech-to-text APIs and not avatar roleplay session products.

## What GA covers

Microsoft lists these receptionist capabilities as generally available:

- **Answer questions** grounded in org-configured FAQ files and URLs  
- **Appointment booking** self-service (set up, reschedule, cancel, review via org integrations)  
- **Route with context** — identify specialist or department, transfer the caller, and give the recipient **AI-generated conversation notes**  
- **Serve after hours** (nights and weekends)  

Learn’s setup docs also mark Teams Phone Agent **generally available** (changelog **September 30, 2026**). Treat “GA” as **Microsoft’s roll-out as stated**—Teams Phone licensing, a resource account, and **Copilot Credits** / spending policy still apply; tenant readiness still depends on those prerequisites ([licensing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-reference-prerequisites-licensing)).

## Languages

Microsoft says natural conversations across **more than 60** supported languages and locales ([blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftteamsblog/help-your-customers-get-answers-and-support-with-teams-phone-agent/4560840)). Automatic Recording transcription still has a known Learn caveat (defaults to English United States regardless of configured language)—so multilingual call handling is not the same claim as perfect multilingual recording STT.

## What stays preview

| Surface | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Built-in **Teams Phone Agent** receptionist | **GA rolling out** (as Microsoft states) |
| **Queues app** supervisor dashboard integration | **Public preview** (Queues app users need **Teams Premium**) |
| **Copilot Studio** custom voice agents (handoff or direct-dial on Teams Phone) | **Frontier / preview** — not the same GA claim |

Microsoft bills Teams Phone Agent with **usage-based Copilot Credits** (duration and capabilities invoked). Custom Copilot Studio voice agents are billed separately under Copilot Studio consumption; preview pricing and availability can change ([blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftteamsblog/help-your-customers-get-answers-and-support-with-teams-phone-agent/4560840); [Copilot Studio integrate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/voice-teams-phone-agent)).

## Who should care

Orgs already on Teams Phone who want an AI receptionist on existing auto-attendant / call-flow lines should start at the [Teams Blog GA post](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftteamsblog/help-your-customers-get-answers-and-support-with-teams-phone-agent/4560840)—lead with the **built-in receptionist GA roll-out**, keep **60+ languages** as Microsoft states it, and keep **Copilot Studio Frontier** and the **Queues dashboard preview** clearly separate.
